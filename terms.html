4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 Service Pricing

WeTow quotes a Service Price for each Job before the User authorises payment. The Service Price is calculated based on:

  • The service type (towing, fuel delivery, jump-start, tyre change, or minor repairs);
  • The distance between pickup and drop-off locations;
  • The equipment required (standard tow, flatbed, or oversized equipment); and
  • The incident classification at the time of booking (see clause 4.4).

The full Service Price is displayed to the User on the booking screen before the User confirms their request.

4.2 Payment Processing Fees

All payments on the WeTow platform are processed through our payment provider, PayFast (Payfast (Pty) Ltd).

A payment processing fee is charged by PayFast on each transaction and is payable by the User in addition to the Service Price. The payment processing fee is as charged by the payment provider from time to time. The applicable fee is shown as a separate line item on the Payment Summary screen before the User authorises payment.

The Total Payment displayed on the Payment Summary screen is the full amount that will be charged to the User's payment method. The User's authorisation of payment constitutes consent to be charged the Total Payment displayed on that screen.

The payment processing fee is remitted in full to PayFast and does not form part of WeTow's revenue or the Driver's earnings. WeTow does not mark up or profit from the payment processing fee.

4.3 Revenue Share

For each completed Job, earnings are divided as follows, calculated on the Service Price only (excluding payment processing fees):

  • Driver: 85% of the Service Price
  • WeTow: 15% of the Service Price (the "Platform Fee")

Payment processing fees paid by the User are separately remitted to the payment provider and are not part of this revenue share.

4.4 Reclassification of Service

At the scene, a Driver may determine that the Job requires reclassification to a higher service tier due to conditions not apparent at booking — for example, accident damage, vehicle recovery from difficult terrain, or other circumstances requiring additional equipment or labour. The reclassification tiers and multipliers applied to the original Service Price are:

  • Breakdown (default): ×1.00 of the Service Price
  • Accident: ×1.40 of the Service Price
  • Recovery: ×2.00 of the Service Price

Where a Driver requests reclassification, the User will be:

  • Notified via the App and given the opportunity to review photographic evidence and a written description provided by the Driver;
  • Shown the revised Service Price and the additional amount payable, inclusive of payment processing fees applicable to the additional payment; and
  • Required to expressly accept or decline the reclassification before the Job continues.

If the User declines the reclassification, the Job is terminated and the User is charged only the originally quoted Service Price. If the User accepts, an additional payment is required before the Driver proceeds with the reclassified service.

4.5 Authorisation of Charges

By confirming a Job or accepting a reclassification, the User authorises WeTow and PayFast to charge the User's chosen payment method for the Total Payment displayed on the Payment Summary screen.
